About Us

Established in 2025, Peterson Auto Solutions is a small local business proudly serving our local community. Our ASE Master Technician brings over 30 years of experience to every repair, ensuring your vehicle receives expert care with a personal touch.

We can handle most any general services and repairs including alternators, batteries, brakes, suspension, lighting, diagnostics, fluid services, and so much more. We offer the most competitive and affordable pricing and turn around times in the area, with same day service available in most cases — because we know you need your vehicle back on the road fast.

Special Services: After hours services and mobile repairs are also available for your convenience.

As we continue to expand, we look forward to offering additional services including tire replacements and alignments.
